About Us

Student Leadership Council (SLC) is a student organization at SMA & SMK Budi Luhur that aims to foster leadership skills, promote student engagement, and organize various events and activities throughout the academic year.

Our mission is to create a vibrant school community where students can develop their leadership potential, collaborate on projects, and contribute positively to the school environment.
